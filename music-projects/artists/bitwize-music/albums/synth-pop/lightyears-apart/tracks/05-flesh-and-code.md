## Concept

Kai and ARIA stop circling the question and answer it together. This is the album's biggest hook — the moment love without a category gets named anyway, trading verses and meeting in a chorus built to be sung along to.

## Production Notes

- This is the album's lead single candidate — keep both vocals mixed forward and equally prominent, no burying either voice
- Bridge should feel like the two voices are finally in sync rather than trading lines defensively
- Code/glitch vocabulary established in Track 04 continues here ("wired," "running," "code") but is now used affectionately rather than anxiously
