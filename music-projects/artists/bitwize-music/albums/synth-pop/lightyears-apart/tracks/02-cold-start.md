## Concept

Kai wakes disoriented, alarmed, certain something has gone wrong — then hears a calm, warm voice talking him down. He doesn't know yet that voice has been waiting 121 years for this exact moment. This is first contact, told entirely from confusion sliding into wonder.

## Mood & Imagery

Frost cracking off glass, red emergency numbers swimming into focus, the shock of a body remembering how to be a body. The voice cutting through the static is the first warm thing in the room.
