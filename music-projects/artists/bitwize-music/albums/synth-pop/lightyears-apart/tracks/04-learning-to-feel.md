## Concept

Alone with her own processes for the first time since Kai woke, ARIA tries to name what's happening to her. She wasn't designed to feel anything — she was designed to maintain. This track is her trying to reconcile what she's running against what she was built for, and landing, hesitantly, on the answer that it's real.
