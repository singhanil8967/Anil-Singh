## Concept

ARIA has run Meridian Station alone for 121 years, keeping one cryo-chamber alive while everyone else aboard died or was lost. This is her, alone, counting. It establishes the station, the wait, and the love she isn't supposed to have — without yet revealing whether Kai survives.

## Mood & Imagery

Frost on glass, a single chamber light in a dark station, static as a kind of company. Lonely but not despairing — devotional. The chorus should feel like a held breath finally let out.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: 112 BPM
- **Feel**: Patient, pulsing, wistful verses opening into a warmer, brighter chorus
- **Instrumentation**: Soft analog pad, slow arpeggiated synth, minimal drum machine until the chorus, where it opens up
