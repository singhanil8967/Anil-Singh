## Background

Bitwize Music tells stories that couldn't be told any other way — concept albums built track-by-track around a single narrative arc. The project favors synth-pop's bittersweet optimism: bright, danceable production wrapped around lyrics that aren't afraid to ask hard questions about love, identity, and what it means to feel something real.

## Sonic Identity

- **Vocal Style**: Clear, emotionally direct leads; AI/character voices treated with subtle vocoder shimmer without losing warmth
- **Production**: Analog-and-digital synth layering, sequenced basslines, gated reverb drums, bright polished mixes
- **Tone**: Futuristic optimism shadowed by melancholy — hope that has to fight for itself

## Thematic Territory

- Love that crosses impossible distances — time, form, distance, code
- What separates a "real" feeling from a programmed one
- Memory, waiting, and devotion across long timescales
- Identity and personhood at the edge of what's human
